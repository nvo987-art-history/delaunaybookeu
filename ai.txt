## Description

DELAUNAY BOOK is a multilingual scholarly publishing initiative
presenting research on colour as a generative and organising
principle in the work of Sonia Delaunay.

The project examines relationships between colour theory,
visual perception, modern art, textile design, digital imaging,
and generative art.
