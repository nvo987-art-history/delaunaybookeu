# DELAUNAY BOOK

**Color as a Generative Principle in the Work of Sonia Delaunay**

An independent publishing initiative by Nicholas Van-Orton.

Official website: https://delaunaybook.eu/

## Overview

DELAUNAY BOOK presents a multilingual scholarly publication
examining the role of colour in the work of Sonia Delaunay.

The project explores colour as a physical phenomenon, a perceptual
experience, a means of artistic expression, and an organising
principle connecting modern painting, textile design, digital
imaging, and generative art.

The publication places colour within a broader interdisciplinary
framework involving art history, visual culture, colour theory,
perception, and contemporary digital technologies.
