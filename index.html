Delaunay Book

A single research project, developed across languages.

Delaunay Book brings together multilingual editions of scholarly research concerned with visual culture, modern and contemporary art, colour theory, perception, material systems and generative artistic structures.

The Editions

One visual language.

The Delaunay Book editions are presented through a unified digital architecture. Typography, spacing, navigation and visual structure remain consistent across languages.

The language changes; the identity remains. This creates a coherent international research platform while allowing each edition to exist independently in its respective linguistic context.
