The Central Idea

Scientific colour theory provides the initial framework. In modern painting, colour becomes increasingly independent of representation.

In Sonia Delaunay's work, relationships between colours become capable of generating movement, rhythm and spatial experience.

The study follows this structural understanding into textile design, digital imaging and, finally, the algorithmic perspective of generative art.
