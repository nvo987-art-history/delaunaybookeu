The Book

Colour is simultaneously a physical phenomenon, a perceptual experience, a means of artistic expression, and a form of technological information.

This book follows the historical development through which colour gradually became an autonomous organising principle.

From scientific colour theory and modern painting to textile design, digital imaging and generative art, the study follows a continuous structural logic.
