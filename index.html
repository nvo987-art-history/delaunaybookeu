The Book

A scholarly study tracing the development of colour from physical phenomenon and perception toward an autonomous organising principle.

The Book

Colour is simultaneously a physical phenomenon, a perceptual experience, a means of artistic expression, and a form of technological information.

This book follows the long development through which colour gradually became an autonomous organising principle. Its trajectory moves from the optical research of Isaac Newton through the changing theories of perception and modern painting, into textile design, digital imaging and contemporary generative art.

The Central Idea

Scientific colour theory provides the initial framework. In modern painting, colour becomes increasingly independent of representation.

In Sonia Delaunay's work, relationships between colours become capable of generating movement, rhythm and spatial experience.

The study follows this structural understanding into textile design, digital imaging and, finally, the algorithmic perspective of generative art.
